About Us ♡

A little space where sweetness brings people together.

Sweet Desserts, Sweeter Stories

Welcome to craVSyu!

A little space where sweetness brings people together. Here, we share various homemade desserts, simple recipes, baking tips and a lot of love for all dessert lovers.

Hello! ♡

Nurul Syuhadah is who I am. I was born in Johor and I am from Pahang. I attend KPTM Ipoh to pursue a Diploma in Digital Business.

In order to make it simple for everyone to learn about sweet desserts, I created craVSyu — a place where I share easy, delicious and enjoyable dessert ideas for everyone to try.

Our Mission

To share easy, delicious, and creative dessert recipes that everyone can try, and to spread the joy of baking to more people.

Our Vision

To become a trusted platform for dessert lovers where they can find inspiration, knowledge, and sweet happiness.

What We Do

We provide a variety of dessert recipes, baking tips, and beautiful ideas to help everyone explore the wonderful world of sweets.
